Experience

The Walt Disney Company

Senior Software Engineer · 2019–present
  • 96 FPS on Meta Quest 3 rendering a 3D environment around a 4K stereo film at 48 FPS, on an XR2 Gen 2 that also runs tracking and passthrough, with about half the peak GPU compute of Vision Pro's M2.
  • Under 200 ms from tap to first frame on average for 800 MB environments on Vision Pro, using GPU-ready ASTC/KTX textures, front-half-first loading and deferred custom-shader compilation.
  • Alien: Earth environment runs dynamic lighting, shadows, skeletal animation, particle simulation, vertex-displacement shaders, anisotropic reflections and volumetric lighting at the same time.
  • Foveated Gaussian splatting: moved a Metal splat renderer onto RealityKit through LowLevelMesh to gain foveated rendering and hybrid rendering with traditional meshes.
  • On-device AI: real-time semantic segmentation with custom PyTorch models on Core ML plus Metal shaders.

Disney+ immersive viewing environments: Apple Vision Pro and Meta Quest

  • Led the effort to bring ILM-built 3D worlds into Disney+ as immersive viewing environments, announced in Apple's WWDC 2023 keynote and live on Vision Pro's launch day; primary engineer on the RealityKit runtime.
  • Brought FX's Alien: Earth environment to Vision Pro with little project structure: got the vendor's models and animation running in headset and hand-authored the dynamic lighting, particles and some of the sound. Featured by Apple in a 2025 Meet with Apple session.
  • Ported the environments to Meta Quest in the new Meta Spatial SDK: rewrote surface and vertex shaders and built the test-bench app used to debug them for Quest and future Meta hardware before Disney+ integration.
  • Re-architected environments into downloadable bundles, moving about 90% of their code out of the Disney+ app.
